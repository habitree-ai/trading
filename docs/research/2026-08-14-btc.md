  3개 쟁점이 미결. 통과 여부가 단기 방향의 열쇠.
  출처: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/08/05/here-are-the-possible-outcomes-for-clarity-right-now) ·
  [Latham 정책 트래커](https://www.lw.com/en/us-crypto-policy-tracker/legislative-developments)

  디지털 증권=SEC, 디지털 상품=CFTC. BTC는 상품 축이라 규제 명확성 수혜.
  출처: [SEC 보도자료](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-30-sec-clarifies-application-federal-securities-laws-crypto-assets)

  출처: [Begich 하원 보도자료](https://begich.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-nick-begich-leads-legislation-establish-strategic-bitcoin-reserve) ·

- **GENIUS Act(스테이블코인법) 이행 진행.** 2025-07 제정, 2026년 OCC·FinCEN 세부 규칙
